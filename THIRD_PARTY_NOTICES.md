# Third-party sources

Input tables and reference annotations are processed inputs to this study, not
new reference databases. They must be cited and used with their original terms.

| Material | Source and attribution | Terms or source documentation |
|---|---|---|
| SILVA 138.2 taxonomy and mapped 16S reference sequences | Quast et al.; SILVA | https://www.arb-silva.de/silva-license-information/ (CC BY 4.0) |
| UNITE mapped ITS reference sequences | Abarenkov et al.; UNITE | https://unite.ut.ee/ (retain original release terms and attribution) |
| TimeTree5 host phylogeny | Kumar et al. (2022) | https://www.timetree.org/ |
| KEGG-derived category tables and annotations | Kanehisa Laboratories | https://www.genome.jp/kegg/legal.html ; https://www.genome.jp/kegg/rest/ (not relicensed under the project's MIT or data license) |

| BugBase, FAPROTAX2 and metaTraits annotations | Respective resources and publications | https://github.com/knights-lab/BugBase ; https://pages.uoregon.edu/slouca/LoucaLab/archive/FAPROTAX2/ ; https://metatraits.embl.de/ |

Raw reads are not included. Newly sequenced reads are available from NGDC/GSA,
BioProject PRJCA066385, accession CRA044477. The source-study accessions are retained
in the curated metadata. Software dependencies retain their own licenses.
