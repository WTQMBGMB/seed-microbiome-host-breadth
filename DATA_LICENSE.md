# Data licensing

results are made available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Cite the associated dataset and paper.

The MIT license covers original project code, not all material in data archives.
Third-party reference sequences, taxonomy, functional annotations, geographic data
and source-study contributions retain their original attribution and terms.
This notice does not replace or expand those terms. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
